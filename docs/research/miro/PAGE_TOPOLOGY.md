# Miro.com → Kanban landing topology

Source: https://miro.com/ (extracted 2026-10-07). Structure/behavior only — Kanban brand and teal tokens apply.

## Visual order (desktop)

1. **Sticky / overlay nav** — Logo, product mega-menu, login, primary CTA
2. **Hero** — Brand-scale H1 (“Human collaboration at the speed of AI”), short support, dual CTAs, full-bleed product visual
3. **Use-case tabs / solutions** — Research, Roadmap & Planning, Diagrams, Workshops (tab or scroll-linked panels)
4. **Social proof strip** — Stats (100M+, 250+, 6000+) + logo cloud
5. **Platform pillars** — AI, Intelligent Canvas, Formats, Blueprints, Security, Integrations
6. **Product deep-dive** — “The AI platform for teamwork”
7. **Help / resources** — Plans, get started, network, community
8. **Footer** — Dense link columns + legal
